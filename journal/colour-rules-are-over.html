Colour Rules Are Over. Colour Feelings Win.

Moving From Colour Theory to Emotional Colour Systems

Milo ReyesBrand Strategist

Complementary wheels are useful. But people don’t feel theory—they feel memory, mood and meaning.

We now start palettes with feelings, not formulas. What should someone feel in the first second? Then we find the colours that carry it.

Our method

  • Mood words → three adjectives everyone agrees on.
  • Reference walk → objects, places, fabrics—not other brands.
  • Real tests → on screens, packaging and in daylight.

Rules still help

Contrast ratios and accessibility are non-negotiable. Emotion leads; rules keep it usable.

The takeaway

Colour is the fastest way a brand speaks. Make sure it says something true.
