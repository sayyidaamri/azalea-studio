Designing Culture in the Age of the Feed

Making Work That Travels Without Losing Its Soul

Sienna HartProject Manager

Visuals now spread faster than the brands behind them. Designing for the feed means designing for remix.

Every image will be cropped, screenshotted and reposted. We design systems that survive that journey instead of single perfect frames.

What travels well

  • One strong shape → recognisable even at thumbnail size.
  • Repeatable motifs → easy for fans to recreate.
  • Honest moments → real beats polished in a busy feed.

Don’t chase every trend

Trends are useful signals, not strategies. Borrow the energy, keep your voice.

The takeaway

Make work people want to share, and make sure it still looks like you when they do.
